How to use this dashboard

A guide for board members — navigation, data interpretation and status indicators.

Last updated:

Welcome to the Kinia Performance & Impact Dashboard

This dashboard tracks Kinia's progress against the 2026–2030 Strategic Plan across three pillars: Deepening Educator Expertise, Driving System-Wide Educational Change, and Increased Financial Independence. It is updated by the Kinia admin team and shared with the Board of Directors.

Data is presented as cumulative totals — for example, "Educators upskilled" shows the total number reached since the start of the plan, not just in the current year. Annual and quarterly breakdowns are provided alongside to give in-year context.

Navigating the dashboard

1
Use the tabs across the top to move between sections: Overview, Quarterly, Insights, Pillar pages, Reach & Influence, Impact Stories, and Risks.
2
Use the year pills (top right of the navigation bar) to switch between years. The selected year determines what targets and actuals are shown on metric cards and KPI bars. Charts always show the full 2024–2030 range for trend context.
3
The Quarterly tab has its own separate year selector and shows in-year progress by quarter (January–March = Q1, April–June = Q2, July–September = Q3, October–December = Q4), plus cross-year comparisons of the same quarter across multiple years.
4
The Insights tab provides auto-generated analysis — efficiency ratios, growth indices, and trajectory charts — that update automatically as data is entered.

Year status — what the colours mean

✓ Closed year (2024, 2025)

The year has ended. Actuals entered are final. RAG status reflects actual performance against the full annual target.

● Current year (2026)

The year is in progress. RAG status compares actuals against the pro-rata expected figure at the current point in the year — not the full annual target.

○ Future year (2027–2030)

Year not yet started. Targets are shown for planning purposes. No actuals or RAG status are displayed.

Understanding metric cards

Each metric card shows three layers of information:

Cumulative target The total expected to be achieved by the end of this year since the plan began (e.g. 32,000 educators upskilled in total by end of 2026).
Annual target The net new figure required in this calendar year alone to stay on track (e.g. +3,000 new educators upskilled in 2026).
Actual The confirmed figure to date. For the current year (2026), this reflects Q1 performance. The RAG status on the card shows whether this is on track.

The card background colour immediately signals performance status — green, amber, or red.

RAG status — performance indicators

RAG (Red / Amber / Green) status is calculated differently depending on whether the year is closed or in progress:

Green — On track

Actual is ≥ 85% of the expected figure. Performance is within acceptable tolerance and on track to meet the annual target.

Current year: compared against pro-rata quarterly pace. Closed year: compared against full annual target.

Amber — Monitor

Actual is 70–84% of the expected figure. Performance is below pace and warrants attention, but may recover by year end.

Board should note and request an update at next meeting.

Red — Action required

Actual is < 70% of the expected figure. Performance is significantly behind pace and requires discussion and remedial action.

Board should seek explanation and agree corrective actions.

Important — current year (2026) RAG explained

Because 2026 data currently reflects Q1 only (January–March), actuals are compared against 25% of the annual target — the expected cumulative figure at the end of Q1. A Q1 actual that is 85%+ of the Q1 expected pace is Green. This avoids incorrectly flagging early-year figures as red simply because the year is not yet complete.

Data currency & update cycle

  • Annual actuals for 2024 and 2025 are final confirmed figures.
  • 2026 actuals currently reflect Q1 performance (January–March 2026).
  • Quarterly data for 2024 and 2025 is estimated by dividing annual totals evenly across four quarters.
  • The dashboard is updated by the Kinia admin team. For questions about data, contact the CEO or Head of Strategy.

Access & download

  • Board viewer PIN: provided separately by the admin team.
  • Admin PIN: held by designated Kinia staff only — not shared with the Board.
  • Use the Download app button (top right of this page or the Overview page) to save a local copy of the dashboard that works offline in any browser.
